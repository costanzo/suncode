10 / Rules

Non-negotiable visual rules

Use these constraints when adding a new module or component.

Conversation first

Keep the central work surface visually dominant. Supporting bays collapse before the conversation becomes cramped.

Status lamp rule

Color must explain an action, a state or a change. Do not add color as decoration.

Visible focus

Every keyboard target keeps a two-step accent focus ring. Never remove focus without replacing it.
